# Asset credits

## Redot brand kit

The complete user-supplied **Redot Engine – Brand Kit (v.1.1)** is copied into
`assets/redot_brand/`. Its original readme and brand guidelines are included.

Redot Engine logo © 2024 Asrorul Irsyad, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The logos retain their original proportions. The player is the Redion logo,
with additional arcade effects drawn around it.

Lato and JetBrains Mono are included from the brand kit, under the SIL Open Font
License. Their original licenses accompany the extracted fonts in `assets/fonts/`.

## Godot ghost mascots

The Godot icon is from the official [Godot press kit](https://godotengine.org/press/).
Godot Engine logo © 2017 Andrea Calabró, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The icons represent the upstream engine in this playful, unofficial arcade game.
Ghost bodies, frightened-state effects, and trails are original additions.

## Other art and audio

Maze geometry, interface, particles, fork pickup, animations, and synthesized
sound effects/music are generated by this project's GDScript code.
